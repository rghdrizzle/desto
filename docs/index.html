Coming soon How it works

The system, in four parts.

Built on two open protocols: nostr for transport and identity, cashu for private payment.

01

Encrypt on your device.

Files are encrypted before they leave. Nodes hold ciphertext and never see what is inside.

02

Pay in ecash.

Cashu tokens pay for storage. Blind signatures keep a payment from being tied to the person who made it.

03

Held by anyone.

Independent nodes on nostr store your data. No company, no central server, no account.

04

Retrieve with a key.

Your nostr key is your login. Fetch your files from any device, any time.

FOR NODE OPERATORS

Run a node. Earn ecash.

Anyone can run a desto node. It connects over nostr and uses it for service discovery, stores encrypted data for users, and gets paid in cashu ecash. No permission, no sign-up.
